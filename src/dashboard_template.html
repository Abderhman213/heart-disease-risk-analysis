Caveat: hospitals differ a lot in who gets referred (Switzerland 93.5% positive vs Hungary 36%), and 172 cholesterol values were recorded as 0 and treated as missing. This is observational data: association, not causation, and not a diagnostic tool.

Data quality · missing values

% of the 920 records missing, after cleaning (impossible zeros counted as missing)

What was cleaned

Steps applied before analysis
1

Merged 4 hospital files (Cleveland, Hungary, Switzerland, VA) into one table of 920 patients.

2

172 cholesterol and 1 blood-pressure values were recorded as 0, which is impossible, so they were set to missing.

3

Slope, vessels (ca) and thal are 34-66% missing, so they were left out of the model rather than guessed.

4

Remaining gaps were filled with the median inside the model pipeline only. Charts use real values.

5

2 exact duplicate rows were kept, since identical readings can occur in different patients.
